Add `wp.gemm()`, a CUDA-graph-capturable GEMM backed by a runtime-loaded cuBLASLt (no link-time dependency) with per-shape plan caching, autotuning, pointer-alignment-aware algorithm selection, and fused bias/ReLU/GELU epilogues including backward (dGELU and bias-gradient) epilogues. Calls without an activation epilogue are differentiable through `wp.Tape` (the adjoint GEMMs and bias reduction are recorded on the tape). `record_tape=False` skips the recording, for callers that record their own backward pass.
