Add `wp.gemm()`, a CUDA-graph-capturable GEMM backed by a runtime-loaded cuBLASLt (no link-time dependency) with per-shape plan caching, autotuning, pointer-alignment-aware algorithm selection, fused bias and ReLU epilogues, and a fused bias gradient for weight-gradient GEMMs. Calls without an activation epilogue are differentiable through `wp.Tape` (the adjoint GEMMs and bias reduction are recorded on the tape). `record_tape=False` skips the recording, for callers that record their own backward pass. CPU arrays, CUDA devices without cuBLASLt and API captures (`apic=True`) use a built-in Warp kernel (recorded in CPU graphs and saved graphs); `wp.is_cublaslt_available()` reports whether cuBLASLt can be loaded, and the `cublas-cu12`/`cublas-cu13` extras install it. float16 and bfloat16 operands can write float32 outputs (mixed precision).
