Add `wp.warp_sum()` and `wp.warp_max()`, warp-level reductions for SIMT kernels: a butterfly of `__shfl_xor_sync` over the 32 lanes of a warp (or over independent segments of `width` lanes) that hands the result to every lane, without the shared memory and block synchronization of `wp.tile_sum()`, so a block can hold several independent groups of 32 threads (e.g. one warp per matrix row). Both are differentiable; on the CPU every thread is its own warp.
